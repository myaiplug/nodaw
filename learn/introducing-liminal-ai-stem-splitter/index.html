Why Local Processing Matters

Cloud stem splitters require you to upload your audio to third-party servers, wait for processing, and often pay monthly or by credit. Liminal shifts that work onto your own machine. Your unreleased tracks remain on your hard drive, processing is not tied to an external queue, and your access does not expire when a billing cycle ends.
